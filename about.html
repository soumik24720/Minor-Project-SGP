ABOUT US

Empowering Futures, Shaping Engineers : Where Innovation Meets Excellence!

Welcome to Siliguri Government Polytechnic College – a premier educational institution dedicated to fostering excellence in technical education. Located in the heart of Siliguri, West Bengal, our college has been serving as a beacon of knowledge and skill development since its inception in 1995. Initially founded as Women's Polytechnic, Siliguri under the guidance of the World Bank Project, the institution was established to empower women and promote quality education in the region. Over the years, we have evolved into a co-educational institution that caters to students from all backgrounds, equipping them with the necessary skills and knowledge to succeed in the ever-changing world of technology and engineering. Our mission is to provide comprehensive, industry-aligned programs that prepare students for careers in various technical fields. With a focus on practical learning and hands-on experience, we offer a wide range of diploma courses in Computer Science and Technology, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering, Civil Engineering, Architectural Engineering, and Pharmacy. At Siliguri Government Polytechnic College, we pride ourselves on our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and a rich learning environment that nurtures both personal and professional growth. We believe in developing not just technical skills, but also the values of discipline, integrity, and leadership. Our college is equipped with modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, and a variety of extracurricular activities that help our students grow beyond the classroom. We emphasize practical training and real-world problem-solving, ensuring that our graduates are industry-ready and equipped to make a difference in their respective fields. We welcome all students with a passion for learning, and we invite you to join us in shaping a brighter, more innovative future!
